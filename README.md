**What Is WorksBuddy?**

WorksBuddy is an AI-powered business automation platform that brings sales, marketing, customer communications, project management, and business operations into a connected workspace. Its specialized AI tools help teams organize work, act on enquiries, manage conversations, and reduce repetitive tasks.
Instead of relying on disconnected apps and manual handoffs, businesses can use WorksBuddy to support workflows from initial lead capture through outreach, appointments, project delivery, and billing.

**Official website:** https://worksbuddy.ai/


**Key Features**

**Lead capture and CRM:** Bring enquiries into a shared pipeline, enrich and score leads, assign owners, and monitor opportunities.

**AI voice conversations:** Answer inbound calls, make outbound calls, schedule appointments, and record call outcomes.

**Multichannel outreach:** Build and manage outreach across supported email, WhatsApp, and AI calling channels.

**Project and task management:** Coordinate tasks, sprints, time logs, deadlines, and project progress.

**AI search visibility:** Understand how brands appear in AI-generated answers and track relevant buyer queries.

**Billing and invoicing:** Create invoices and manage billing workflows.

**Electronic signatures:** Prepare and track document signature workflows; verify current availability on the product website.

**Workflow automation:** Connect business steps and reduce repetitive manual handoffs.

**How WorksBuddy Fits into a Business Workflow**

**A typical sales-to-delivery process may look like this:**

**1. Capture:** Lio organizes incoming enquiries and routes leads to a responsible team member.

**2. Engage:** Evox helps the team run outreach and follow-ups over supported channels.

**3. Connect:** Ciao helps handle voice conversations and appointment booking.

**4. Deliver:** Taro keeps project tasks and responsibilities visible after a deal progresses.

**5. Bill:** Inzo helps teams prepare and manage invoices.

**6. Automate:** Revo can coordinate repetitive steps between workflows where supported.

This is an illustrative workflow, not a guarantee that every step is enabled by default.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Is WorksBuddy a CRM?**
WorksBuddy includes Lio, its CRM and lead management tool, alongside other tools for outreach, calling, project management, and operations.

**Does WorksBuddy offer AI voice automation?**
Yes. Ciao is WorksBuddy's AI voice agent for supported inbound and outbound calling workflows.

**Can WorksBuddy help with project management?**
Yes. Taro provides task and project management capabilities.

**Does WorksBuddy support outreach automation?**
Evox supports outreach workflows across selected email, WhatsApp, and AI voice channels; feature availability depends on configuration.

**Is WorksBuddy related to Tencent's WorkBuddy AI?**
No. WorksBuddy at worksbuddy.ai is a separate product and should not be confused with Tencent's similarly named WorkBuddy AI.
